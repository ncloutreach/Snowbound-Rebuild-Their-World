The game is designed on the principle of experential and visual learning. Jigsaw puzzles strengthens spatial reasoning, problem-solving skills, and memory by requiring children to recognize patterns and assemble images. Coupling this with quiz questions leverages the science of retrieval practice – method proven to deepen knowledge retention. The integration of climate change-related questions ensures that children not only engage with the puzzle image but also connect it to real-world scientific scenarios such as greenhouse gas emissions, melting ice and endangered habitats. This dual approach merges cognitive development with environmental education.
