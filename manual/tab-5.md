1.	What is causing the polar bear’s ice to shrink?

A)	Climate change.

B)	Weight of polar bear.

C)	Rains.

D)	Using ice slides.

Correct Answer: A) Climate change

Explanation: The ice is melting because of climate change.


2.	Why do polar bears need sea ice to live?

A)	For playing games.

B)	To hunt seals and travel.

C)	For camping.

D)	To keep their fur clean.

Correct Answer: B) To hunt seals and travel.

Explanation: Ice acts as hunting ground for polar bears to hunt seals and also to travel.


3.	What happens to polar bears when the sea ice melts too quickly?

A)	Their population grows.

B)	Finding food will be difficult for them.

C)	Their eyes become green.

D)	They become happy.

Correct Answer: B) Finding food will be difficult for them.

Explanation: They have to swim long distances and find it harder to find food.


4. What is one main reason for the ice caps melting?

A)	Cold drinks.

B)	Global warming.

C)	Too much snow.

D)	Weight of polar bears.

Correct Answer: B) Global warming

Explanation: The Earth is getting warmer because of too many greenhouse gases.


5. What can we do to help stop the ice from melting?

A)	Reduce the global warming.

B)	Do not touch the ice.

C)	Nothing.

D)	Be optimistic.

Correct Answer: A) Reduce the global warming.

Explanation: Reducing the global warming stops the ice from melting.
